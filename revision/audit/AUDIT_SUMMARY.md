## What the audit verified and what it did not

Verified: the data flow from the raw daily frames to the 958 labelled days, the temporal cut, the train-only fit of imputer, SMOTE and scaler, the label rule, the persistence and majority baselines, the shared bootstrap matrix and both skip rules, the extended metrics and paired comparisons, the calibration and safety-layer figures, the threshold grid, the SHAP reference and its aggregation, the group ablation, the label-agreement statistics, the default and tuned model figures and the search space, the expanding-window folds and pooled figures, the parser's treatment of the `source` field in the raw exports, and each numerical sentence of the manuscript, response letter and supplementary that the six briefs named.

Not verified: the full T2.2 cross-validation (27.6 min) and any TabNet or FT-Transformer fit (the committed CV and test tables were checked for internal consistency and the tuned tree models through their prediction files); the repeated-run procedures (T1.4 SHAP stability runs, T2.1 model seeds) were not re-executed in full; the Health Auto Export documentation and the export application's aggregation rule (F4.4); the content of the raw exports beyond the `source` field and the sleep dates; the manuscript text beyond the targeted numerical claims (prose, references, figures as images, the sections inherited from the base manuscript); the register entries of tasks outside the six briefs (for example T4.x and the figure files); and the authors' text decisions in `AUDIT_DECISIONS.md`, which is untracked and was read only for the open question handed to F6.

General conclusion in one sentence: the revision's numbers are reproducible and were reproduced, including the unfavorable ones (temporal robustness, model hierarchy, calibration); the findings concern how four of them are described (provenance of the Watch features, the LightGBM configuration, the two previous-day conventions, the bootstrap skip rules), two text figures that are wrong at the printed precision (F3.1, F6.1), and two design choices whose pre-run fixation cannot be shown from git (F4.3, F6.2).
