Response. We assessed the calibration of the Random Forest probabilities with out-of-fold probabilities from a temporal cross-validation on the training period, fitted Platt and isotonic calibrators on them, and evaluated on the test set (ECE, MCE, multiclass Brier score, log loss, reliability diagram, and the Active class separately). The raw forest is over-confident (top-label ECE 0.138 [0.082, 0.207]); calibration reduces ECE to 0.036 (Platt) and 0.041 (isotonic) with intervals excluding the raw value. Calibration does not, however, improve the system, and we report why. In every bin of the raw Active probability the observed fraction of Active days is between 3% and 17%, so the probability does not separate the minority class, and any monotone calibrator maps it towards the base rate. Under calibrated probabilities the argmax is Normal on every test day, and the safety layer never issues a Rest or Active recommendation. We therefore keep the safety layer on raw probabilities, report calibration as a result and as a limitation (weak discrimination of the Active class), and state that the reported confidence should not be read as a frequency.

In the course of this analysis we identified an error in the original Section 4.9. The figures given there for the confidence threshold (80% coverage, mean caloric error 76 to 69 kcal) had been produced with an earlier 16-feature pipeline, not with the reported model. The correct figures on the reported model at the 0.50 threshold are 65.6% coverage (66 of 192 days falling back to Normal), mean caloric error 71.9 kcal without threshold and 58.3 kcal with it, and maximum error 200 kcal (unchanged). We have corrected the section and added the states issued at the threshold (28 Rest, 162 Normal, 2 Active). We also note that the mean caloric error must be read together with the states issued, since a system that always recommends Normal has a lower mean error (54.2 kcal) precisely because it never issues a Rest or Active recommendation.
